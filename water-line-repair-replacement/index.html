The pipe between your meter and your house

A water service line is the buried pipe that carries water from the meter box, usually near the street, to where it enters your home. Water line repair fixes a leak or break in that pipe. Replacement installs a new line when the old one has failed along its length.

In most cases the homeowner owns this section. The water supplier handles the meter and its side of the connection. That makes a leak in the yard your repair, even though the water is lost before it reaches the house.

Leaks here are easy to miss. The water soaks into the soil rather than showing up indoors, so the first sign is often a higher bill or a patch of ground that stays wet.

Signs of a leaking water service line

These point outside the house rather than inside.

  • 1The meter moves with the house main closed. If the leak indicator keeps turning with the house main shut, the leak is on the service line.
  • 2A wet or soft spot in the yard. Ground that stays damp or sinks between the meter and the house is a classic sign.
  • 3Low pressure throughout the home. A significant leak can drop pressure at every fixture.
  • 4Water in the meter box. Standing water around the meter can mean a leak at the connection.
  • 5A bill jump with no new use. Water lost in the yard still passes through the meter.

Why West Valley service lines leak

Soil conditions play a part. Hard caliche layers and shifting fill around newer homes can stress buried pipe and fittings over time.

The IPC also generally requires the water service line to be kept 5 feet from the building sewer, which is why replacement routes are planned before digging. Pressure matters too. High street pressure without a working regulator at the house puts constant strain on the service line and every fitting inside. Keeping pressure in range protects the whole system.

Roots and landscaping round out the list. Irrigation trenches and tree roots near the line can damage it, which is one reason to know where the line runs before planting.

The line from your meter to the house is yours whether EPCOR or the City of Surprise supplies the water. In this part of Maricopa County, caliche layers near Grand Avenue and the Agua Fria corridor make buried lines harder to reach and repair.

Insurance and service lines

Many policies exclude the service line itself. Some insurers and utilities offer service line coverage as an add-on, which may pay for repair or replacement.
