A 24/7 plumber built around how Surprise homes are plumbed

An emergency plumber is a plumbing technician who responds outside normal hours to stop an active leak, restore water or drainage, and make the system safe until a full repair is done. In Surprise, a Maricopa County city northwest of Phoenix, that call usually involves a slab-on-grade house, a water heater in a hot garage, and water from one of several suppliers.

Surprise grew from 30,848 residents in 2000 to 117,517 in 2010, according to the U.S. Census. That building boom means much of the city's plumbing is now 15 to 25 years old. Original shutoff valves, supply lines, and first or second water heaters are reaching the age when they start to fail, so a quick fix today often prevents a flooded floor next summer.

Our line runs 24/7 because those failures ignore business hours. A braided supply line under a vanity in Sun City Grand, or a tank that splits in a Marley Park garage, can put hundreds of gallons on the floor before sunrise. The first thing we do on every call is talk you through shutting off the water. Then we set the visit and give you the price before anything is opened up.

If the trouble is a slow or backed-up line, start with our drain cleaning and unclogging service. For anything that drips, cracks, or quit working, the general plumbing repair page lists the everyday fixes we handle across Surprise.

Drain cleaning and unclogging

Kitchen lines clog from grease and food scraps more than anything else. The City of Surprise even runs a Be G.R.O.S.S. campaign asking residents to keep fats, oils, and grease out of the sewer for exactly that reason. Bathroom lines clog with hair and soap film.

A cable machine sized to the pipe clears most blockages. When a line clogs again within weeks, a camera shows whether roots, a sag, or a broken joint is the real cause. Our drain unclogging page explains when snaking is enough and when a line needs a stronger method.

One drain or all of them?

One slow fixture points to a local clog. Several at once, especially the lowest tub or toilet, points to the main line.

General plumbing repair

This covers the everyday failures: a dripping shutoff valve, a cracked PVC trap, a leaking angle stop under a vanity, or a toilet that runs all night and doubles the bill.

Many 1990s and early 2000s homes around Kingswood Parke and Sun City Grand still carry their original builder-grade valves, and those are among the first parts to wear out at that age. The plumbing repair service page walks through each fix and what it involves.

Find the nearest shutoff

Most fixtures have a small valve behind or below them. A quarter turn stops that fixture without cutting water to the whole house.

Water softener installation and repair

EPCOR's Agua Fria district blends Central Arizona Project river water with Lake Pleasant water and groundwater from 15 wells. The city's Mountain Vista system pumps groundwater from 16 wells. Two homes a few miles apart can have noticeably different water.

Published hardness figures for Surprise disagree, sometimes by a factor of three or more. That is why we test the water at your tap before sizing or programming anything. See our softener installation and repair service for how that test and setup work.

Who bills you for water?

The name on the bill tells you which system serves the house. Each supplier publishes a yearly water quality report with a hardness figure.

Water heater repair and replacement

Most Surprise water heaters live in the garage, where summer air runs far hotter than inside the house. Heat plus mineral scale shortens tank life and wears out valves, elements, and thermostats.

A tank leaking from the bottom is finished, and installing a new water heater is the realistic fix. If the unit only heats poorly, repairing the existing heater by replacing an element, thermostat, or gas valve may be enough.

Check this first

Read the data label

The serial number usually encodes the build year. A tank past about 10 years that starts leaking is almost always replaced.

Leak detection

A water meter that keeps moving with every fixture off is the clearest sign of a hidden leak. In slab homes the leak can be under the foundation, inside a wall, or on the service line between the meter and the house.

Acoustic listening gear, thermal imaging, and line pressure tests narrow the spot before anything is opened. Our leak detection service in Surprise covers each method and when it is used.

Two-minute test

Watch the meter

Turn off every tap, then watch the small leak indicator on the meter face. If it still turns, water is going somewhere you cannot see.

Sewer line repair

A sewer problem shows up as gurgling toilets, a sewage smell near a floor drain, or water rising in the lowest tub when the washer drains. Roots, grease, and settled joints are the usual culprits.

A camera goes down the line first so the fix matches the damage. Sewer line repair handles cracks and offset joints in one spot, while a lateral that has failed along its length needs full replacement.

Check this first

Locate your cleanout

Most homes have a capped cleanout outside near the front of the house. A clear, reachable cleanout lets a plumber open the main without pulling a toilet.

The first five minutes of a plumbing emergency

These steps limit the damage no matter who you call. They take less time than waiting on hold anywhere.

  • 1Shut off the main. In many Surprise homes the main valve sits where the service line enters, often in the garage near the water heater or on the wall facing the street. Find it now, before you need it.
  • 2Turn off the water heater. Set a gas unit to pilot or switch off the breaker for an electric one, so it does not heat an empty tank.
  • 3Drain the pressure. Open a faucet at the lowest point in the house, such as a tub spout, to empty the lines faster.
  • 4Keep clear of wet outlets. If water is near an outlet, cord, or appliance, turn off that circuit at the panel before stepping in.
  • 5Photograph everything. Take pictures of the source and the wet areas before cleanup. Your insurer will ask for them.
