Have these ready when you call

A two-minute call goes faster with a few details in hand. None of them are required, but each one helps get the right plumber and the right parts to your door.

  • 1Your address and ZIP. We cover 85374, 85378, 85379, 85387, and 85388 inside Surprise, plus El Mirage, Sun City West, Youngtown, Waddell, and Wittmann.
  • 2What you see and hear. Water on the floor, a hissing wall, a gurgling toilet, a meter that keeps moving. Plain words are fine.
  • 3Whether the water is off. If it is still running, we will walk you to the main valve first.
  • 4Equipment details. For a water heater or softener, the brand and the data label photo help. For a leak, note roughly how long it has been going.
  • 5Who can let the plumber in. Renters and snowbird owners should say who has keys and whether a landlord or HOA needs notice.
