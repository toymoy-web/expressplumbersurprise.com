Emergency burst pipe repair, day or night

Burst pipe repair is stopping and fixing a pipe or fitting that has split or failed under pressure, so water can be turned back on safely. A burst supply line can release a large volume of water in minutes, which is why speed matters more than anything.

Our emergency line answers 24/7. The first minutes of the call are about getting the water off. Once it is off, the plumber finds the failed section, repairs it, and pressure-tests the line before restoring service.

Many Surprise homes are 15 to 30 years old. At that age, supply lines to toilets, sinks, and appliances are common failure points, along with fittings in hot attics and lines under constant high pressure.

What to do right now if a pipe has burst

Do these in order. Every minute of running water adds to the damage.

  • 1Shut off the main. It is usually where the water line enters the home, often in the garage or on the street-facing wall.
  • 2Turn off the water heater. Switch gas to pilot or cut the breaker so it does not heat an empty tank.
  • 3Cut power near the water. Turn off circuits for any outlets or appliances that are wet, if you can reach the panel safely.
  • 4Open a low faucet. Draining the lines relieves pressure and empties water out of the system.
  • 5Photograph and start drying. Take pictures for insurance, then move belongings and start removing standing water.

After the leak is stopped

Once the pipe is repaired, the line is pressure-tested and water is restored. The plumber also checks whether the failure was a one-off or a sign of a wider problem, like a failed pressure regulator or aging lines throughout the house.

Drying out the structure is a separate job from fixing the pipe. Wet drywall, flooring, and cabinets need proper drying to prevent mold, which is the work covered on our water damage restoration page.
