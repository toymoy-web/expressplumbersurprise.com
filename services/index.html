Find the right plumbing service for your Surprise home

All work in Surprise is held to the 2024 International Plumbing Code (IPC), which the city adopted effective January 1, 2026. Plumbing problems rarely arrive labeled. You notice a slow drain, a warm floor, or a tank that rumbles, and the right service depends on what is behind it. This page groups our services by symptom so you can find the one that fits.

If you are not sure, call (623) 301-9042. The 24/7 line can talk through what you are seeing and point you to the right fix, including whether it can safely wait until morning.

Drains and sewer

Slow drains, backups, and sewer lines.

  • Drain cleaning

    Clogged sinks, tubs, and main lines are cleared and tested with drain cleaning and unclogging, available 24/7.

  • Hydro jetting

    Grease-coated and root-prone lines are scoured clean with high-pressure water jetting after a camera check.

  • Sewer line repair

    Cracks, roots, and offset joints are fixed with spot repairs and lining for damaged laterals, from spot fixes to lining.

  • Sewer line replacement

    Laterals that have failed along their length are replaced through a full lateral replacement.

  • Septic tank pumping

    Rural properties on septic get septic pumping and service plus help with backups.

  • Garbage disposals

    Jammed, humming, or leaking units are handled by garbage disposal repair and installation.

Water heaters

No hot water, leaks, and upgrades.

  • Water heater repair

    Pilots, thermocouples, elements, and valves are fixed with 24/7 water heater repair.

  • Water heater replacement

    New gas, electric, heat pump, and tankless units are sized and set with water heater installation.

  • Tankless service

    Descaling and error-code repairs are part of routine tankless upkeep.

Leaks and pipes

Hidden leaks, burst lines, and aging pipe.

  • Leak detection

    High bills and hidden leaks are traced with non-invasive leak detection.

  • Slab leaks

    Leaks under the foundation are fixed with slab leak detection and repair, by spot fix or reroute.

  • Burst pipes

    Split lines and failed fittings are stopped with emergency burst pipe repair.

  • Repiping

    Homes with repeat leaks get new PEX or copper through whole-home repiping.

  • Water line repair

    Leaks between the meter and the house get fixed through water line repair and replacement.

  • Gas lines

    New appliance runs and leak repairs are covered by gas line installation and repair.

Fixtures and everyday repairs

Faucets, toilets, showers, and general fixes.

  • General plumbing repair

    Valves, supply lines, traps, and pressure issues are fixed through everyday repair visits.

  • Toilets

    Running, leaking, and rocking toilets are handled by toilet repair and installation.

  • Faucets and sinks

    Drips, low flow, and under-sink leaks are fixed with faucet and sink repair.

  • Showers and tubs

    Conversions and anti-scald valves are part of shower and bathtub installation.

Water quality

Hard water, taste, and treatment.

  • Water softeners

    Softeners sized from a tap test come with water softener installation and repair.

  • Water filtration

    Reverse osmosis, carbon, and well systems are chosen through a filtration setup matched to your water.

  • Backflow testing

    Annual testing and repairs are handled with backflow testing and prevention.

Outdoor, pool, and commercial

Beyond the walls of the house.

  • Outdoor faucets

    Dripping spigots and vacuum breakers are fixed with hose bib and outdoor faucet repair.

  • Pool leaks

    Pools that drop faster than evaporation get pool leak detection and repair.

  • Commercial plumbing

    Restaurants, offices, and rentals are served through plumbing service for businesses.

Water damage

Cleanup and drying after the leak is fixed.

  • Water damage restoration

    Extraction, drying, and repairs are covered by drying and rebuild work after a leak.

  • Water mitigation

    Stopping the spread and drying out comes first, through fast extraction and structural drying.
