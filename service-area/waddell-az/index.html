An emergency plumber for Waddell

Waddell calls range from a split fitting in a new Cortessa home to a full septic system on an acreage lot. Our emergency plumber dispatch covers both around the clock, and on bigger parcels the opening task is usually locating a shutoff that sits far from the home.

Before we arrive in Waddell: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

Rural lots add a few extra steps to the usual emergency routine.

  • 1Shut off the main. On acreage, the main shutoff may be at the meter far from the house, so know where it is before you need it.
  • 2Kill power to wet areas. Switch off circuits near the leak at the panel, and do not step into standing water to reach it.
  • 3Cut the well pump if needed. Well owners can kill the pump at its breaker, which stops the water feeding a broken line.
  • 4Stop using drains during a septic backup. Every flush sends more water into a full system.
  • 5Clear the area, animals included. Move stored items away from the water and keep livestock away from pooling water near stalls or pens.
  • 6Call our 24/7 line. Give gate codes and landmarks so the plumber can find a rural home in the dark.

White Tank Mountain Regional Park and summer storms

Waddell sits beside White Tank Mountain Regional Park, at 29,271 acres the largest regional park in Maricopa County. Runoff from the mountains during monsoon storms can wash across lots and roads.

After a heavy storm, check septic lids and cleanouts for washed-in debris and watch for sudden slow drains. Storm water can also soak around a tank or drain field and slow it down for a while.
