An emergency plumber for Litchfield Park

Litchfield Park looks nothing like the desert tract housing around it, and its pipes age differently too. Our emergency plumber dispatch heads south from Surprise around the clock, and stopping the flow comes before anything else.

The city began in 1916, when Goodyear Tire and Rubber bought farmland here to grow long-staple cotton for tire cord. The project was led by Paul Litchfield, and the company town took his name in 1926. Goodyear ran it for roughly 70 years before the city incorporated in 1987, and according to the 2020 census it was home to 6,847 people.

That long history left a mix of housing. Homes near The Wigwam and the original town core sit a few streets from 1960s and 1970s planned villages and newer homes, all inside about 3.3 square miles.

Mature trees and sewer lines

Litchfield Park is known for green, established landscaping, including the grounds of The Wigwam, which covers about 440 acres. Large, old trees are beautiful, but their roots look for water, and an older sewer lateral with a loose joint is an easy target.

If drains in an established home keep slowing down every few months, roots are a likely reason. A camera inspection shows where they enter, and sewer line repair seals the joint so the problem does not keep coming back.

Before we arrive in Litchfield Park: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

Do these first, in this order.

  • 1Shut off the main valve. In older homes the valve often sits on the street-side wall or in the meter box.
  • 2Kill power to wet areas. Turn off the breakers serving wet outlets or appliances, as long as the panel is dry and you are not standing in water.
  • 3Clear the area. Move rugs, furniture, and stored items away from the water and keep pets out of the room.
  • 4Do not force an old valve. Original gate valves can be brittle. If the house valve will not turn, use the one at the meter.
  • 5Call our 24/7 line. Tell us the cross street nearest Litchfield Road or Indian School Road and who can open the door.
