Common plumbing work in Sun City West

Leaks are the top concern. In older homes, pinholes in copper and failures in polybutylene supply lines can appear in different rooms over a few years. When that pattern starts, whole-home repiping is often worth discussing instead of patching each leak.

Accessibility upgrades come up often too. Many residents replace a tub they no longer use with a low-threshold shower. Our tub-to-shower conversion service covers those projects along with modern anti-scald valves.

Water heaters and toilets round it out. Swapping a worn toilet for a comfort-height model, or replacing a tank that has passed 10 years, is steady work across the community. For toilets, see toilet repair and installation.

Seasonal residents and summer shutoffs

Plenty of Sun City West owners head north for the summer. A failed supply line in a closed-up house may not be found until a neighbor spots water at the door.

Before leaving, shut off the main valve, turn the water heater to vacation or pilot, and have a friend walk through after big monsoon storms. In attached homes and duplexes, a leak can also reach the unit next door, so a shutoff protects neighbors too.

Before we arrive in Sun City West: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

Take these steps while a Sun City West emergency plumber is on the way. Ask a neighbor for help with any of them if you need it.

  • 1Close the main shutoff. Most homes have it where the water line enters, often in the garage. A stiff valve can be hard to turn, so it is fine to ask a neighbor.
  • 2Kill power to wet areas. Cut the breaker for any outlet or appliance near the water, but only if the panel is dry and easy to reach.
  • 3Clear the area and keep your footing. Wet tile is slippery. Move rugs and stored items away from the water and walk carefully.
  • 4Warn the unit next door. In duplexes and attached homes, water can travel through a shared wall, so let your neighbor know.
  • 5Call our 24/7 line. Give your street and nearest cross street so the plumber finds you quickly, even in the middle of the night.
