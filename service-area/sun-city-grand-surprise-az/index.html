Plumbing service for Sun City Grand homeowners

Sun City Grand is an age-restricted community inside the City of Surprise. Its community website says it covers 4,000 acres, with 9,550 individual homes and 252 condos. If you need a plumber for a home or condo there, call (623) 301-9042.

The same website says the community is not gated. When you call, give your street and the nearest cross street, and mention any access instructions from your association so the plumber can find the house without delay.

Wikipedia reports that the community was founded in 1996 and that its last homes were completed in 2005. It describes Sun City Grand as a Del Webb community. That puts most homes between about 20 and 30 years old today, which is the main reason this page focuses on parts that wear out with age.

Plumbing services we provide in Sun City Grand

The most common reasons to call are a water heater that is leaking or not heating, a toilet that keeps running, a slow or backed-up drain, and a leak you cannot find. Our water heater repair and leak detection pages explain how each repair works.

Condo and villa owners can call too. If your association handles part of the building, tell us which pipes are yours and which belong to the association, and we will work on the ones that are yours.

If a repair turns out to be larger than a single part, the plumber explains the options and the price before starting. That includes replacing a whole water heater rather than patching an old one.

If water is spreading in your home right now

Take these steps while you wait for the plumber.

  • 1Shut off the main valve. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Know where it is before you need it.
  • 2Stop using drains. If a drain is backing up, every flush or sink use adds to the water on the floor.
  • 3Switch off power near the water. Only do this if you can reach the panel without standing in water.
  • 4Call the line. Call (623) 301-9042 and tell the dispatcher what you see and where the shutoff is.

Association rules and which utility serves you

Sun City Grand is run by a Community Association, and its published fee schedule includes an architectural review fee for buyers. Ask the association before changing anything that can be seen from outside, such as moving a hose bib or running a new outdoor line.
