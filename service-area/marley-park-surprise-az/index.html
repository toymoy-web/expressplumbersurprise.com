Plumbing services we provide in Marley Park

Common requests include a water heater that is leaking or not heating, a toilet that keeps running, a slow kitchen sink and a leak that shows up as a water stain. Our drain cleaning and faucet and sink repair pages explain those repairs in detail.

Townhouse and condo owners should tell the dispatcher which pipes belong to the home and which belong to the association. We work on the pipes that are yours and explain what the association needs to handle.

If you are adding an outdoor kitchen, a casita bath or a new fixture, ask the plumber about it during the visit so the work can be priced together.

If water is spreading in your home right now

Take these steps while you wait for the plumber.

  • 1Shut off the main valve. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Townhouse owners should also know whether a building shutoff exists.
  • 2Stop using drains. During a backup, every flush adds to the water on the floor.
  • 3Warn neighbors. In an attached home, tell the neighbor on the other side of the wall if water may reach them.
  • 4Call the line. Call (623) 301-9042 and tell the dispatcher what you see.
