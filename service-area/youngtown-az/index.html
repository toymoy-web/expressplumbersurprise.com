An emergency plumber for Youngtown

An emergency plumber in Youngtown often starts by finding a shutoff that has not moved in decades. Rusted gate valves, brittle galvanized lines, and old cast iron drains are part of the job here. Dispatch into this compact town runs around the clock, so most streets are reached quickly.

Youngtown has a place in history. In 1954 developers bought 320 acres of farmland from Frances Greer's ranch, and Ben Schleifer designed the country's first planned community built just for retirees. By 1955, 125 homes stood on the land, most of them small houses around 750 square feet.

The town incorporated in 1960 and later opened to all ages. According to the Arizona Commerce Authority, its population doubled between 2000 and 2010 as the Agua Fria Ranch planned community was built, so Youngtown now has two very different sets of plumbing.

What older Youngtown plumbing needs

Homes from the 1950s often still have some original piping. Galvanized steel supply pipe rusts from the inside, which shows up as low pressure and brown water. Cast iron drains can crack or scale up over decades.

The sewer lateral from an original home is also decades old. Repeat backups in an older house deserve a camera look, and our sewer line repair service covers what that inspection can find.

When leaks start showing up in more than one place, it may be time to replace the supply lines instead of patching them. The repiping page explains how that works, and burst pipe repair handles the emergency in the meantime.

That sewer and repipe work does not stop at the town line. Across the Agua Fria River it reaches 85335 in El Mirage, and to the northwest it runs into Surprise ZIPs 85374 and 85379.

Youngtown's water comes from EPCOR, whose Sun City district also serves the town. A pressure question or a leak at the meter goes to EPCOR, and anything past the meter on your property is a plumbing repair.

Before we arrive in Youngtown: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

Start here, whether the house is an original from the 1950s or a newer Agua Fria Ranch build.

  • 1Find the main shutoff. In older homes it may be outside near the front of the house or at the meter.
  • 2Turn the valve gently. Old gate valves can be stiff or brittle. Turn slowly and do not force it.
  • 3Use the meter valve if needed. If the house valve will not close, the meter valve stops water from the street.
  • 4Kill power and clear the area. Switch off circuits near the water if the panel is dry, and move furniture and stored items out of the way.
  • 5Turn off the water heater. Put a gas heater on pilot. Electric units get switched off at the panel.
  • 6Call our 24/7 line. Tell us whether the house is original or newer so the plumber brings the right fittings.
