If water is spreading in your home right now

Take these steps while you wait for the plumber.

  • 1Shut off the main valve. Turn it clockwise until it stops. In many Arizona homes it is on an exterior wall or in the garage.
  • 2Turn off the water heater. If the tank itself is leaking, shut off its supply valve and its power or gas control.
  • 3Move belongings. Lift rugs and electronics off the floor and keep children and pets away from the wet area.
  • 4Call the line. Call (623) 301-9042 and tell the dispatcher what you see.
