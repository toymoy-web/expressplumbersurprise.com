Plumbing service for the Original Town Site

The Original Town Site is where Surprise began. The City of Surprise history page describes the original town as a one-square-mile area bordered by El Mirage Road on the east, Bell Road on the north, Dysart Road on the west and Greenway Road on the south. Call (623) 301-9042 if you need a plumber there.

Wikipedia says Surprise was founded in 1938 by Flora Mae Statler and that the townsite was subdivided to build inexpensive houses for agricultural workers. It reports that Surprise incorporated on December 12, 1960.

We could not find published home counts or build years for this area, so this page makes no claim about what pipes are in any house. What it offers is a straightforward approach for older parcels that have been remodeled over many decades.

If water is spreading in your building right now

Take these steps while you wait for the plumber.

  • 1Shut off the main valve. Turn it clockwise until it stops. If it will not turn, tell the dispatcher so the plumber arrives prepared.
  • 2Stop using drains. During a backup, every flush adds to the water on the floor.
  • 3Switch off power near the water. Only do this if you can reach the panel without standing in water.
  • 4Call the line. Call (623) 301-9042 and tell the dispatcher what you see.
