Plumbing calls we see most in El Mirage

Clogged kitchen and main lines lead the list, especially in rentals where many different people have used the drains. A backup that hits the lowest tub first is a main-line problem, and our drain cleaning service clears it and runs a camera if it comes back.

Water heaters come next. Most El Mirage homes keep the tank in the garage, where summer heat and mineral scale shorten its life. A tank that leaks from its base has reached the end, while a unit that only heats poorly may need a water heater repair instead of a new tank.

Hidden leaks round it out. Warm spots on tile, a hissing sound, or a bill that jumped with no change in use point to a leak under the slab, which is where slab leak detection and repair comes in.

Military moves, rentals, and empty houses

Being next to Luke Air Force Base means many El Mirage homes change hands or tenants often. Homes can sit empty between moves, and a small leak in a vacant house can go unnoticed until the next tenant walks in.

Before we arrive in El Mirage: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

These steps limit the damage while an El Mirage emergency plumber is on the way.

  • 1Shut off the main valve. Look where the water line comes into the house. In El Mirage tract homes that is often a garage wall. Turn it clockwise until it stops.
  • 2Kill power to wet areas. If water is near outlets, the washer, or the water heater, switch those circuits off at the panel, but only if you can reach it without standing in water.
  • 3Clear the immediate area. Move rugs, electronics, and anything stored on the garage floor away from the water, and keep kids and pets out of the room.
  • 4Stop using drains during a backup. Every flush adds to the water coming back up, so hold off until the line is open.
  • 5Call our 24/7 line. Give your cross streets near El Mirage Road, Thunderbird Road, or Grand Avenue, and tell us who can let the plumber in.
