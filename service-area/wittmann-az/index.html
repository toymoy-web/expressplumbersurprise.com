Common plumbing calls in Wittmann

Septic systems are the biggest difference from city homes. A tank that has not been pumped in years eventually backs up, and the fix starts with septic tank pumping and a look at the drain field.

Water heaters are close behind. Many rural homes run a propane water heater, and a pilot that will not stay lit or a gas valve that has failed can leave a family without hot water. Water heater repair covers both gas and propane units.

Well water can be hard on fixtures and filters. Sediment and minerals clog aerators and valves, so many owners add water filtration matched to a lab test of the well.

Before we arrive in Wittmann: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

The drive out takes longer than a call in town, so these steps count for more on a rural property.

  • 1Shut off the well pump. If a line bursts, switching off the pump stops the water supply.
  • 2Close the house shutoff. Many rural homes have a valve where the line enters the house, separate from the well.
  • 3Cut the power and move things. If a dry breaker panel is within reach, turn off the circuits near the leak, then carry belongings out of the water's path.
  • 4Stop using drains in a septic backup. Every flush adds to a full tank.
  • 5Turn off the propane to the water heater. If the heater is leaking or you smell gas, shut the supply and leave if the smell is strong.
  • 6Call our 24/7 line. Give road names, gate codes, and landmarks from Grand Avenue so the plumber can find you.
